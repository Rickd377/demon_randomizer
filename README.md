<h1>Demon Randomizer</h1>
<p>This mod gets all your completed demons. Based on your choices you get generated an uncompleted demon level with your picked difficulty to play.</p>
<hr>
<h3>Here is what options you can choose from:</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Demon Difficulty</li>
  <li>A generated demon level based on your skills</li>
  <li>A generated demon level not based on your skills</li>
</ul>

<h3>Reasons to use this mod:</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Complete demons faster</li>
  <li>Play demons that are more fun to play</li>
  <li>Improve at skillsets you are not very good at yet</li>
</ul>

<h4>Instructions:</h4>
<ol>
  <li>From the main menu, click the creator tab</li>
  <li>Navigate to the level search page</li>
  <li>Click the button with a demon face button on the right of the screen</li>
  <li>Adjust the difficulty settings as you please</li>
  <li>Click the button "Personalized" or "Challenge Me" to get your random level</li>
<ol>
